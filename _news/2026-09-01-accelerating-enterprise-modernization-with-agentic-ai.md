Join us this September at **PyData St. Louis** for an engaging session on how agentic AI is changing the way enterprises modernize legacy software.

[RSVP today on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-st-louis/events/316295374).

Enterprises today sit on mountains of legacy code—from decades-old mainframe systems and monolithic .NET applications to complex database architectures that resist modular change. Traditional modernization efforts can take months or years of manual work while organizations face a shrinking pool of engineers familiar with these systems.

What if AI agents could reason about, plan, and execute these transformations at enterprise scale?

In this session, **Saurabh Sharma** will walk through **AWS Transform**, an agentic AI service purpose-built for enterprise modernization. We'll explore how specialized AI agents can collaborate to analyze legacy workloads, decompose monoliths, transform code across languages and frameworks, and continuously address technical debt.

## In this session, we'll cover

- How agentic AI differs from traditional rule-based migration tools, with agents that can reason, plan, and adapt
- How AI agents can help analyze and transform large legacy codebases
- Real-world modernization patterns, including mainframe → cloud-native transformations
- .NET modernization and custom code, API, and framework transformations, including Python
- How specialized agents can collaborate across different stages of a modernization project
- How "continuous modernization" can turn technical-debt remediation from a one-time project into an ongoing capability
- The broader role of LLMs and agentic systems in large-scale software engineering
