Join us **tomorrow, Tuesday, October 13**, for an engaging conversation about how generative AI is changing the work of data analysts.

[RSVP today on Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/pydata-st-louis/events/316703010).

Generative AI is increasingly capable of performing tasks that have traditionally been central to data analytics, from writing code and cleaning data to creating visualizations and generating insights. In this talk, **Cassandra “Cassie” Collier, Ph.D.**, Assistant Professor of Computer Management and Information Systems at Southern Illinois University Edwardsville, will explore how AI is reshaping analytics work, which tasks may be most affected, and what skills are likely to become more important.

Drawing on published research with data analytics professionals and hiring managers, Cassie will discuss how organizations and analysts are responding to AI-led disruption, where human expertise remains critical, and how data professionals can prepare for an evolving profession.
